## Left illustration prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create a tall narrow vertical margin illustration for an academic researcher's website, portrait aspect 1:3. Genuine transparent background, dark warm charcoal ink only, no white backing, no shadows, no colored fills. Charming thoughtful hand-drawn editorial doodles, clear rounded human faces, simple confident lines legible at 120px width. Four loosely separated small scenes stacked vertically with generous transparent space, linked by a very delicate meandering dotted pencil trail: top two people conversing with small speech bubbles containing single characters A, अ, আ (no other text); next a little globe with dotted travel paths and two people looking at it, no national borders or flags; next three equal-status South Asian people of varied ages and clothing, one wearing a headscarf, one short-haired with glasses, one with curly hair, sharing an open book, no stereotypical roles; bottom a person holding two speech cards with differing scripts and a small magnifying glass beside the cards, suggesting multilingual bias evaluation. Inspired by research comparing sociocultural associations across languages, regional South Asian intersectional biases, and identity cues. No ranking of cultures, no caricatures, no nationality labels, no robot, no charts, no borders, no heading. Artwork should fill narrow width with visible simple subjects, not tiny dense detail. Save as transparent PNG asset.

## Right illustration prompt

Use case illustration-story. A tall narrow 1:3 vertical margin doodle for an academic researcher website. Genuine transparent background with dark charcoal pen outlines, no white backing no shadows no color fill. Cute but thoughtful editorial hand-drawn human scenes with rounded clear faces and consistent medium-weight simple strokes. Three distinct vertically stacked scenes with ample empty transparent gaps linked by a thin meandering pencil trail. Top: a researcher with magnifying glass inspecting two small framed pictures of the SAME two people in different everyday contexts, a workplace and a park, with question marks suggesting questioning visual stereotypes; depict varied hairstyles, a headscarf and glasses without any negative caricature. Middle: two people receive identical little achievement certificates, an observer considers a small lightbulb and a die in two thought bubbles, representing competing talent-versus-luck explanations of the same success; do not associate either explanation with a particular demographic person. Bottom: people from two groups at equal height cooperating to assemble a little bridge, one uses a wheelchair, all equal active contributors; a tiny open psychology notebook nearby, representing intergroup contact, cooperation, common goals and bias mitigation. No words, no labels, no robot, no frames enclosing scenes, no border. Maintain readable silhouettes when shrunk to 120px wide. Make one continuous vertical margin illustration, not a webpage mockup. Transparent PNG.
